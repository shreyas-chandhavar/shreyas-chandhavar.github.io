Engineering workflow

From aerodynamic reference to load path.

The project was built as one traceable chain rather than as disconnected CAD and simulation exercises. The NACA0012 profile defines the geometric context, the control-surface load model generates the hinge demand, analytical sizing establishes the first structural baseline, and SolidWorks Simulation resolves the local effects around the pin/lug/bracket interfaces.

1 · Reference geometry300 mm NACA0012 profile generated from the standard four-digit thickness equation.
2 · Hinge definitionHinge axis located at 225 mm from the leading edge, corresponding to x/c = 0.75.
3 · Load screeningVelocity and deflection are converted into control-surface force, hinge moment and horn force.
4 · Analytical sizingPin shear/bending, lug bearing, net section and bracket bearing establish nominal margins.
5 · CAD load pathSolidWorks assembly maps the pin, lug, bracket, spar and horn into a physical mechanism.
6 · FEA correlationLocal contact FEA is compared against the nominal analytical stress rather than treated as an isolated result.
System definition

A public geometry with an original hinge study.

The aerodynamic context uses a non-proprietary NACA0012 section with a 300 mm chord. The control-surface break is placed at 75% chord, leaving a 75 mm control-surface chord. The hinge mechanism is an original portfolio load-path demonstrator rather than a reconstruction of proprietary aircraft hardware.

Analytical → FEA

Nominal sizing first. Local stress second.

The maximum screened condition was 50 m/s and 25° control deflection. With the project load model and a 1.5 structural load factor, the design hinge reaction is 322.33 N.

What the comparison means

FEA is not being used to decorate the CAD.

The analytical model answers the first sizing question quickly and transparently. The FEA model then asks a different question: where does the real geometry concentrate the load once pin/lug/bracket contact is represented?

The result is a 1.65× increase from nominal analytical pin stress to local FEA peak stress. That difference is kept in the project rather than tuned away because it illustrates the practical role of local simulation after hand/analytical sizing.

The final investigated static configuration remains above the project screening target FoS of 3.0 within the stated model assumptions.

Claim boundary

What this project does not prove.

This is a portfolio-level engineering study, not flight-hardware substantiation or certification evidence. It does not include fatigue or spectrum loading, full airframe/control-surface flexibility, detailed fastener preload, tolerance sensitivity, nonlinear material behaviour, certification load cases or a documented mesh-convergence study.

The hinge assembly demonstrates the mechanical load path and analysis workflow. It is not claimed to be a packaging-optimized internal hinge installation inside a real NACA0012 wing.

A lightweight 2-D SU2 aerodynamic check was used during the broader workflow, but the exact original SU2 configuration, mesh and coefficient-history files were not available in the final publishing workspace. No solver output has been fabricated to fill that gap.

Reference basis: the NACA0012 geometry is based on the public NACA four-digit airfoil definition and NACA Report 460. SU2 is referenced only as an open-source aerodynamic workflow used during the project.
