# Siemens NX concept model

The NX model is a **concept-level packaging and geometry visualization**, not a stiffness-certified structural design.

Modelled elements:
- AGARD 445.6 NACA 65A004 wing geometry
- nominal mid-span passive-reinforcement region (35–55% semispan in the deterministic concept)
- separate local reinforcement surface/solid representation
- conceptual 60 × 10 × 152.4 mm active-actuator packaging envelope

The final ML/physics-selected candidate shifts the optimized reinforcement region to approximately 36.5–56.5% semispan. The NX model therefore represents the nominal deterministic architecture rather than a final manufacturing definition. No proprietary aircraft geometry or actuator design is used.
